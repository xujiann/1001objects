Made specifically for the tomb, this unglazed storage jar displays the animals of the four directions (dragon, phoenix, tiger, snake-entwined tortoise) in high relief around the neck. Around the belly appear the 12 animals of the zodiac, standing in correct order: rat, ox, tiger, hare, dragon, snake, horse, ram, monkey, cockerel, dog, and pig.
