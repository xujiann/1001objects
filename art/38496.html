These ornately decorated lacquer objects are the cores of handheld drums used in performances of Noh theater and Kabuki. To these cores would have been affixed a drumhead of animal hide. The lacquer decorations are in the so-called “kōdaiji” style, which was popular in the sixteenth and seventeenth centuries.
