Make-do teapot

佚名 (Anonymous)

年代 Date
c. 1720
媒材 Medium

馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
中国 / China

This teapot probably lost its original porcelain spout in transit. Objects with such repairs such as this are often called make-dos. They illustrate the extent to which even broken pieces of Chinese Export were highly prized, that they would be custom-fitted with silver rather than discarded.
