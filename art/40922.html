Vessel in the form of a man

Nasca artist

年代 Date
3rd century BCE–6th century CE
媒材 Medium
Ceramics
馆藏 Collection
明尼阿波利斯艺术学院 / Minneapolis Institute of Art
地区 Region
秘鲁 / Peru

This man wears face paint and a turban that are typical of Nazca adornments. Although his body is shapeless, his teeth are finely detailed, as is the trophy head in his hands. The painting surrounding the vessel's lower half is an elegant rendering in many colors of fish, starfish, and octopuses. Carefully preserved heads have been found in Nazca burials. Shown in art throughout the Nazca era, they are called trophy
