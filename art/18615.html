非洲 / Africa

Mbuun men wove and embroidered wrap skirts like this for women to wear on special occasions. Gently color-shifted patterns ( lubawa ) along the central panels were achieved by “floating” wefts (selectively covering over vertical, or warp, threads with horizontal, or weft, threads).
